description=A first-generation Renault Trafic for [b]Project Zomboid Build 42.21[/b]: short wheelbase, low roof and a panel cargo body without rear side windows. A modest work van to haul your supplies through Kentucky.
description=
description=[h2]My first vehicle experiment[/h2]
description=
description=This is [b]my first attempt at making a vehicle for Project Zomboid[/b], while trying to control Blender through a coding agent. The vehicle was generated from [b]official vehicle blueprints[/b], without using any existing 3D model. The body, interior, wheels and animations were built for this project.
description=
description=It is an experiment that I intend to keep improving. Feedback on its appearance, handling and everyday use is welcome.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Original model of the 1985 Renault Trafic I T800 panel van.
description=[*]Two front seats and a cargo capacity of 120.
description=[*]Animated front doors, right sliding cargo door, twin rear doors and hood.
description=[*]The same cargo storage is accessible through the rear doors or the open sliding door.
description=[*]Clean bodywork in white or blue: approximately 70% white and 30% blue when vehicles are created.
description=[*]Modelled interior, textured wheels and transparent windscreen and front door glass.
description=[*]Natural vehicle spawns in parking, delivery and trades zones, as well as junkyards.
description=[*]Vanilla vehicle mechanics and loot. No other mod is required.
description=[/list]
